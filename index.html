Research Paper

A comparative analysis of verbalized confidence and behavioural consistency as uncertainty signals in LLMs. Evaluated LLaMA 3.1 8B Instruct on 600 questions across arithmetic reasoning, commonsense reasoning, and domain knowledge tasks — finding behavioural consistency outperforms verbalized confidence across all metrics, and that their disagreement pattern reveals two distinct failure modes: false confidence and articulation failure.
